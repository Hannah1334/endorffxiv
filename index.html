The Opal Raen



Race: Au Ra - Raen
Endor’s complexion is noticeably pale. Her skin and hair lack pigment, contrasted by light reflecting a rainbow of vibrant color off her white scales. It’s common to hear reference to her as “The Opal Raen” with scales nearer to the gemstones of their namesake than they are to typical Au Ra—a trait inherited from her mother’s bloodline.

Pallid coloring is confined to a petite frame. She stands no taller than 4’9” and weighs far too little for someone her age. Though the typical hourglass shape often attributed to the female form is plenty present in her silhouette, there’s something off about how small her wrists and ankles are.

If one were to assume Endor to be ill, they’d be correct. Another genetic inheritance provided by her mother is an unknown illness with several visible side effects: albinism to varying degrees, and an inability to gain significant weight or muscle mass. The disease is known to drastically shorten the lives of her forebears, and reduce their quality of life to greater extremes the longer they survive.
Despite this, the young Raen maintains an active lifestyle and a fulfilling practice in arcane artistry.

Family History



As a child born from a traditional marriage, Endor is given her father’s surname. The Dalah clan is a long-standing Raen family that began in the Azim Steppe long ago, before the Raen tribes began migrating away from their Xaela kin. Contributions to the arts, and investments in developing trade routes, solidified their name in the annals of history while amassing them a great fortune. High expectations join hands with greedy politics in their ways of life.

Equally as intriguing, her mother’s surname bears a storied history of its own. The Iteya clan, another old Raen family, was known to produce exceptional warriors. Ninjas and Samurais born among them were comparable to prodigies with skills unmatched. Endor’s mother herself–Sume Iteya–was a Samurai in service to the Doman Royal Family before Garlemald’s invasion.

Sume bore ten children for her husband. Endor Dalah was the last to be born before her mother’s illness escalated drastically and sentenced her to a life of pitiful confinement. As the only child to inherit her mother’s condition, Endor was deemed her caretaker from a very young age. The two spent all their time together, garnering a favoritism that isolated her from the rest of her siblings through growing resentment.

When Garlemald began expanding on their significant presence in Hingashi, Sume panicked and made the brash decision to ship her youngest to Eorzea–where the empire that brought her homeland to heel had yet to find success in their many attempts.

Endor does not openly discuss her family or her physical condition, but she’s happy to share how she managed to survive the last twelve years in Limsa Lominsa.

Carbuncle Appreciator



Endor displayed an incredible understanding of intricate patterns and mathematics at a young age. At only ten namedays she manifested her carbuncle for the first time. The youth was immediately attached to her arcane companion, naming it Ume and showering it with immeasurable love and care. To this day her bond with Ume remains, and together they’ve managed to accomplish many notable feats.

Daughter of The Spinner



Feeling alone and vulnerable on a new continent at such a young age, Endor found more comfort in Eorzean faith than in the teachings of her homeland. She forged a particularly strong bond with The Spinner, naming Nymeia as her patron deity. A fascination with Astrology naturally followed, and Endor desperately sought knowledge of the mysterious magics. Her threads of Fate were spun in crossing with that of a Sharlayan Viera named Cenra Shev, who mentored her in the art.
